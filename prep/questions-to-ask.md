# Questions to Ask Interviewers

## About the Role

- What does a typical day or week look like for this role?
- What are the biggest challenges the person in this role will face in the first 6 months?
- How is success measured for this position?
- What's the balance between building new features vs maintaining existing systems?
- How much autonomy does this role have in choosing technical approaches?

## About the Team

- How is the engineering team structured? How big is the team I'd be joining?
- What does the development process look like — sprints, kanban, something else?
- How does the team handle code reviews and technical decisions?
- What's the on-call setup like?
- How do engineers collaborate with product and design?

## About the Technology

- What does the tech stack look like? Any major migrations planned?
- How do you approach technical debt?
- What's the testing and deployment process — CI/CD, staging environments?
- Are there areas where you're investing in AI/LLM tooling?
- How do you handle incident response and post-mortems?

## About the Company

- What's the company's current growth stage and trajectory?
- How does engineering influence product direction?
- What's the biggest technical bet the company is making right now?
- How has the engineering culture evolved over the past year?

## About Growth

- What does career progression look like for engineers here?
- How does the company support professional development?
- Are there opportunities to lead projects or mentor other engineers?
- What does the path from this role to the next level look like?

## For Specific Stages

### Recruiter Screen
- What's the interview process and timeline?
- What's the compensation range for this role?
- Is the role open due to growth or backfill?

### Hiring Manager
- What are your top priorities for the team this quarter?
- What would you want this person to accomplish in their first 90 days?
- What's your management style?

### Final Round
- What concerns, if any, do you have about my fit for this role?
- What's the decision timeline?
